Dead Bug Coach

Counts your reps and checks form — lower back lifting off the floor, hips rocking, moving too fast. The back-arch check reads the silhouette, not the skeleton, because no pose landmark sits on the spine. Needs a side view.

Train with the camera

Lie on your back, side-on to the camera, whole body in frame, floor visible. Stay still for the first few seconds — that is the floor calibration. Then do three easy reps to set your personal baseline; everything after that is scored against it.
